# Results

All numbers on this page were produced by running the committed scripts in this repository. The primary run uses the committed sample panel, so every table here reproduces offline with:

```
python scripts/analyze.py
python scripts/compare.py
```

The machine-readable copy of the primary run is `results/metrics.json`.

## Panels

| Panel | File | Days | Span | Maturities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Committed sample | `data/treasury_sample.csv` | 400 | 2024-12-03 to 2026-07-10 | 12 (1 Mo to 30 Yr) |
| Full download | `data/treasury_yields.csv` (via `scripts/download_data.py`) | 880 | 2023-01-03 to 2026-07-10 | 12 (1 Mo to 30 Yr) |

Both panels are daily US Treasury par yield curve rates, public domain US government data. The sample is the most recent 400 rows carved from the full download.

## Experiment 1: PCA explained variance

PCA on the mean-centered panel, no scaling, loadings in yield units.

| Component | Interpretation | Sample panel (400 d) | Full panel (880 d) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PC1 | Level | 0.6656 | 0.7741 |
| PC2 | Slope | 0.2775 | 0.1738 |
| PC3 | Curvature | 0.0412 | 0.0406 |
| First three, cumulative | | 0.9843 | 0.9884 |

Three components explain over 98 percent of daily yield curve variance on both panels. The split between level and slope shifts with the window, level dominates more over the longer 2023-2026 span, but the qualitative shapes of the three loading vectors are the same: PC1 loads with one sign across maturities, PC2 changes sign between short and long tenors, PC3 is a butterfly with positive wings and a negative belly.

## Experiment 2: reconstruction error ablation, PCA vs NMF, k = 1..6

Root-mean-square reconstruction error in yield points at matched component counts. PCA reconstructs from k centered components plus the mean curve; NMF factorizes the raw nonnegative panel.

Sample panel (400 days):

| k | PCA RMSE | NMF RMSE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.1436 | 0.1705 |
| 2 | 0.0592 | 0.0972 |
| 3 | 0.0311 | 0.0425 |
| 4 | 0.0186 | 0.0362 |
| 5 | 0.0137 | 0.0348 |
| 6 | 0.0095 | 0.0516 |

Full panel (880 days):

| k | PCA RMSE | NMF RMSE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0.2459 | 0.3703 |
| 2 | 0.1181 | 0.1297 |
| 3 | 0.0556 | 0.0681 |
| 4 | 0.0365 | 0.0531 |
| 5 | 0.0208 | 0.0586 |
| 6 | 0.0146 | 0.0593 |

Observations:

- PCA wins at every matched k on both panels, as theory requires: PCA with its mean term is the optimal linear rank-k approximation in the least-squares sense.
- PCA error decreases monotonically in k. NMF error does not: on the sample panel it rises from k = 5 to k = 6, and on the full panel from k = 4 onward it is roughly flat then worsens. The NMF objective is nonconvex and the multiplicative-update solver lands in local optima at larger k, reported as observed (NNDSVDA initialization, seed 0, 1000 iterations).
- At the economically interesting k = 3, the NMF penalty relative to PCA is modest, about 0.011 yield points RMSE on the sample panel, which is the price paid for nonnegative, directly interpretable basis curves.
